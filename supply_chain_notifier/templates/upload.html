Data Format Guide
Shipment Data

Columns: shipment_id, product, supplier_name, ship_date, expected_delivery_days, actual_delivery_days, delay_days, quantity, status

Inventory Data

Columns: product, warehouse, current_stock_level, normal_stock_level, daily_demand, days_of_supply, stock_status

Supplier Reports

Columns: supplier_id, supplier_name, on_time_delivery_rate, quality_score, defect_rate, baseline_reliability

Privacy: All data is processed locally. No supplier-identifiable data is sent externally (unless GenAI is enabled for summarization).
